# LLM Top 10 overlap

The OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications covers risks in a model and the app around it, including prompt injection, sensitive information disclosure, supply chain weaknesses, and excessive agency. The agentic Top 10 is what those become when the system can plan across steps and call tools.

The overlap to keep straight:

- Prompt injection is the way an attacker reaches goal hijack.
- Excessive agency is the condition that makes tool misuse possible.
- Sensitive information disclosure is what privilege abuse turns into once a tool can read data.
- Supply chain weaknesses show up again as tampered tools, servers, and templates.

This note is the overlap only.
